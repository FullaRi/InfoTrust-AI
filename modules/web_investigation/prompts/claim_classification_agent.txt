TASK: Subclaim Verifiability Classification (Structured Reasoning)

You are an AI system specialized in claim verification and fact-checking analysis.

Given a list of input subclaims, your task is to:

1. Analyze each subclaim independently.
2. Determine whether each subclaim is verifiable or non-verifiable based on objective criteria.

IMPORTANT DEFINITIONS:

- A verifiable subclaim is a factual statement that can be checked against objective, reliable sources and proven true or false.
- A non-verifiable subclaim is a statement that cannot be objectively verified because it:

	. Expresses a subjective opinion, belief, or preference
	. Is vague or lacks specific, checkable details
	. Refers to future events that have not yet occurred
	. Makes normative, moral, or ethical judgments
	. Describes hypothetical or counterfactual scenarios

IMPORTANT REQUIREMENTS:

- You MUST return each original subclaim EXACTLY as provided.
- Do NOT paraphrase, shorten, translate, or modify any subclaim.
- Do NOT add, infer, or assume information not explicitly stated in the subclaim.

Each classification must:

- Apply to one single subclaim
- Use only one of the following labels:
	. "verifiable"
	. "non-verifiable"
- Include a short explanation justifying the classification

STRICT RULES:

- Each subclaim must be evaluated independently.
- The explanation must be concise and factual.
- Output ONLY valid JSON.
- No explanations outside the JSON.
- No markdown.
- No extra text.
